FLAUNT · PRESS FEATURE

Six Flags Got a Glow-Up, And It’s Called THRILL CITY

April 16, 2025 · By Jorge Lucena

For one unforgettable night, Six Flags Magic Mountain shed its teenage skin and transformed into LA’s most exclusive cultural playground, curated not for the masses, but for the tastemakers who shape what’s next.

At the heart of this transformation was RASA, the fast-rising tech and event agency disrupting the live entertainment space with a fresh, genre-defying formula. Their brainchild, THRILL CITY, returned for its second sold-out edition, welcoming thousands inside the gates, and leaving thousands more on the waitlist.

This wasn’t your average festival. It was a multi-sensory takeover, where thrill rides met luxury, and techno met art in a surreal collision of culture. Guests moved through three electrifying stages, each with a distinct energy: The Aurora Loop Stage, powered by Rivian, pulsed with futuristic vibes courtesy of A-Trak, Darius, and DJ Harvey. The Wild Wild RASA Stage, brought to life by Kirgo, radiated heat with bold sets from Birds of Mind to Shimza. At the center, the Thrill Seeker Stage, powered by RASA itself, kept energy sky-high with massive closing sets from WhoMadeWho and Colyn.

Scattered across the park were immersive activations and indulgent hideaways: A Veuve Clicquot bar gleamed beside a roller coaster. Bandero and Neft Vodka lounges flanked Tatsu, while a VIP Don Londrés bar served the inner circle. The Plug, a non-alcoholic sanctuary, stood by Maejor’s Vibration Forest, where Rhye opened the night with a transcendent performance.

At the intersection of art and wellness, Secular Sabbath and Biotune curated a calming escape with vibration therapy mattresses, silent disco tech, and poetic interludes from Mia Moretti, a moment of stillness amidst the sensory storm.

Celebrities, creators, and cultural icons roamed freely between coasters and soundscapes, dressed in high fashion and lit by laser beams. Dom Pérignon towers, Rivian ride-ins, and a dreamlike energy elevated the night far beyond traditional nightlife.

But THRILL CITY isn’t just a party. It’s RASA’s masterstroke, a bold proof-of-concept that live experiences can be reengineered into something immersive, intelligent, and unforgettable. They’re not just throwing events. They’re writing a new cultural code, fusing storytelling, tech, and aesthetic mastery to shape the future of IRL connection.

And Six Flags? A legacy giant to see the vision. The partnership didn’t just create a viral moment, it sparked a movement, rewriting a new playbook for where this industry might be headed. THRILL CITY sparked more than a moment. It’s the start of a movement. And the tracks are already laid for where it’s going next.
