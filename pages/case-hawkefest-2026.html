Case study · Santa Monica Pier, Santa Monica, CA

Hawkefest 2026: A Renaissance Takeover of the Santa Monica Pier

A Renaissance takeover of the Santa Monica Pier, knights, castles, and the Pacific Ocean, for the founders, CMOs, and operators building the future of commerce.

Overview

Client
Hawke Media — Hawkefest 2026
Location
Santa Monica Pier, Santa Monica, CA
Audience
Founders, CMOs, and brand builders
Scope
Full-scale event production, creative direction, venue transformation, thematic design, immersive activations, entertainment, after-party, and onsite operations across the Santa Monica Pier.

Upcoming Event · In Production

RASA is producing Hawkefest 2026, Hawke Media's flagship marketing and business event, transforming the iconic Santa Monica Pier into an immersive Renaissance world on October 1st.

Set against the Pacific Ocean, this year's theme, knights, castles, and the spirit of reinvention, breaks away from the traditional conference circuit to deliver the "anti-conference" Hawkefest is known for. Recognized by Forbes as one of the top business conferences leaders should attend, the event brings founders, CMOs, and brand builders together at the intersection of commerce, marketing, and innovation.

The 2026 program runs from noon to 5pm, flowing into an after party on the Pier until 8pm, and explores the trends, technologies, and strategies, from AI to evolving consumer behavior, shaping the future of commerce. Building on editions like "Hawke Island," "Wild West," and "Magic," and on last year's 800+ attendee gathering, Hawkefest 2026 expands across the full Santa Monica Pier footprint to welcome an even larger community of builders.

Highlights

What we delivered

  1. 01

    Full transformation of the Santa Monica Pier into an immersive Renaissance world, with the Pacific Ocean as its backdrop.

  2. 02

    Themed entertainment, immersive activations, and interactive experiences across the entire Pier footprint.

  3. 03

    A full-day program (12–5pm) flowing seamlessly into an after party on the Pier (5–8pm).

  4. 04

    The signature "anti-conference" format, real conversations, discovery, and unexpected moments over generic panels.

  5. 05

    High-level programming on the trends, technologies, and strategies defining the future of commerce.

  6. 06

    A curated lineup of founders and operators, with 2026 speakers including Shadi Bakour, building on past voices such as LA Rams President Kevin Demoff, Match Group CEO Spencer Rascoff, and Anthony Anderson.

  7. 07

    An expanded footprint scaling beyond last year's 800+ attendees, targeting 1,500+ attendees for 2026.
