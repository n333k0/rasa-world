Case study · Los Angeles

Honey Birdette x Playboy's 'Club Honey'

A luxury Halloween showcase that blurred the line between fashion, nightlife, and cultural influence.

Overview

Client
Honey Birdette x Playboy
Location
Los Angeles
Audience
Over 700 guests including top models, influencers, celebrities, and press
Scope
Full-scale event production, creative direction, red-carpet management, talent relations, guest experience, ticketing, and onsite operations

RASA produced Honey Birdette x Playboy's 'Club Honey', an invite-only, exclusive, Halloween fashion event celebrating Honey Birdette's newest collection.

The takeover of Keys Nightclub transformed the venue into a provocative, high-fashion nightclub experience inspired by the glamour and allure of modern sensuality. Models, influencers, and tastemakers were personally gifted Honey Birdette's collection to wear on the red carpet, creating a bold and immersive showcase that blurred the line between runway, nightlife, and brand experience.

The evening featured press and media coverage, live DJ performances, and custom brand environments designed to amplify Honey Birdette's aesthetic of empowerment, confidence, and luxury. The event served as both a fashion debut and a cultural influence, positioning 'Club Honey' as one of Los Angeles's most talked-about Halloween events.
