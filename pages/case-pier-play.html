Case study · Los Angeles

Pier Play at The Santa Monica Pier

A landmark return of live music to the Santa Monica Pier, reigniting community and culture.

Overview

Client
RASA
Location
Los Angeles
Audience
Local community, city officials, law enforcement, visitors, and partner brands
Scope
Full-scale event production, creative direction, entertainment programming, city coordination, community engagement, and on-site operations

RASA World Group, Inc. presented the concept of bringing live music back to the Santa Monica Pier and worked closely with the Santa Monica Pier Foundation and the City of Santa Monica to create the first music event on the Pier in over five years, marking a meaningful moment in the city's cultural revival.

With a vision to reignite the spirit of one of the world's most iconic venues, RASA designed an experience centered on community, creativity, and safe celebration. The event balanced bold artistic ambition with civic responsibility, demonstrating that music can unite people in a positive, well-managed, and joyful way.

This landmark event became both a cultural milestone and a trust-building moment, highlighting RASA's commitment to seamless execution, collaboration with city officials and law enforcement, and its belief in the transformative power of music.

Highlights

What we delivered

  1. 01

    First live music event on the Santa Monica Pier in over five years.

  2. 02

    Full-scale production including stage design, lighting, sound, artist coordination, and event logistics.

  3. 03

    Strong collaboration with the Santa Monica Pier Foundation, city officials, and law enforcement to ensure safety and compliance.

  4. 04

    Community-driven programming featuring local musicians, food vendors, and family-friendly activities.

  5. 05

    Smooth guest flow, strong turnout, and highly positive onsite feedback from attendees and city stakeholders.

  6. 06

    Successful real-time coordination with city services and Pier operations teams.

Impact

What it changed

  1. 01

    Reestablished the Santa Monica Pier as a premier cultural and entertainment destination.

  2. 02

    Strengthened trust with local government, law enforcement, and community organizations through safe, well-executed event management.

  3. 03

    Boosted economic activity for Pier vendors and nearby businesses.

  4. 04

    Elevated public perception of the Pier as a vibrant, community-centered gathering space.

  5. 05

    Positioned RASA as a preferred event partner for future civic and cultural programming.

  6. 06

    Laid the foundation for an ongoing series of music and cultural activations, expanding access to arts and entertainment in Santa Monica.
