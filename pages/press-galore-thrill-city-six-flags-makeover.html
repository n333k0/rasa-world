THRILL CITY: The Makeover Six Flags Didn't Know It Needed

April 18, 2025 · By Galore Girl

What do you get when you mix Dom Pérignon, designer fits, and a drop tower? THRILL CITY—the wildest, hottest, most Galore-worthy party of the year, where the rave hits 80 MPH and the fashion is as loud as the bass.

On March 22, breakout events-culture-tech brand RASA gave Six Flags Magic Mountain a major makeover. Gone were the traditional park treats, replaced by a world of fashion and fantasy. Three stunning stages. Fifteen world-class DJs. Whomadewho, A-Trak, Rhye, Shimza, Colyn, Darius—the lineup alone was enough to leave you speechless. The atmosphere? Pure magic: lasers, coasters, couture, and an electric crowd swaying under the stars.

Spotted in the madness? Celebs, creatives, and the nightlife elite. Heated VIP lounges. Dom Pérignon & Veuve Clicquot champagne fountain bar.. Rivian on display as art. Tech company, Kirgo, gifted dragon plushies at Tatsu. Private VIP tables. It was giving luxury. It was giving adrenaline. It was giving new era of nightlife.

And here's the tea: this wasn't just a party—it was RASA's full-blown statement. They're turning iconic spaces into culture moments, and Six Flags just got the makeover of the century. No more mascots. No more kiddie rides. Just vibes, beats, and champagne-fueled fashion.
