OUR STORY

Atelier is the event services division of RASA, a global collective crafting transformative experiences that blur the line between art and entertainment. At RASA we combine software, design, and cultural insight to build unforgettable moments and measurable connection for global audiences and brands.

THE ORIGIN

We grew up doing this for ourselves before we did it for anyone else

RASA started as a small crew throwing 300-person gatherings in living rooms and lofts. We are now a decade on, and we've produced more than 200 events, from members-only salons to 10,000-person festivals in San Francisco, LA, Miami, New York, London, Milan, Dubai, and Paris.

Along the way we built the systems, the team and the technology to run at that scale without losing the intimacy and purpose. Atelier is the service we offer brand leaders asking for the same thing we've mastered and do best: building an incredible room, a talented team, and the tech, built especially for their audience.

Everything we recommend, has been tried and tested on our own real events first. You inherit the optimized playbooks, and we partner with you to dream your biggest dreams, so we can make it happen.

ONE ECOSYSTEM

Three ways the world of RASA works

There are three arms to our live culture operating system. Each one feeds the other: our own trend-setting event experiences, our corporate events studio, and our technology platform.

RASA CREATES

RASA's Experiences

The proving ground

The flagship festivals and members-only gatherings we produce ourselves at every scale, from 300-person salons to 10,000-person takeovers, across global hotspots such as San Francisco, Milan and Dubai. Where our creative playbook is proven, live, every weekend.

ATELIER BY RASA

Corporate Events

The playbook, opened up

Atelier is our client-serving event studio. We bring the same team, creative direction, and production standards to corporate brand activations, launches, summits and hospitality. We engineer it to be a unique cultural moment, rather than a business event.

TABULAH BY RASA

The Technology

The intelligence layer

Tabulah by RASA is the audience engagement platform we built to run our own events. It includes a CRM, ticketing, check-in, loyalty and post-event analytics, all unified into one guest profile your brand owns outright.
