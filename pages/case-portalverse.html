Case study · Los Angeles

Portalverse: L.A. Comic Con's Official Closing

L.A. Comic Con's first-ever official closing — a three-floor immersive nightlife takeover where cosplay met club culture.

Overview

Client
L.A. Comic Con
Location
Los Angeles
Audience
L.A. Comic Con (150K+) attendees, content creators, cosplayers, industry guests, and VIPs
Scope
Launch and produce the official closing including full-scale production, multi-floor design, talent sourcing, entertainment booking, vendor management, ticketing, and media coverage.

L.A. Comic Con partnered with RASA World Group, Inc. to produce the convention's first-ever official closing, Portalverse, a fully immersive nightlife experience that extended the energy and creativity of the convention beyond the show floor.

Hosted at the historic Bob Hope Patriotic Hall, the event brought together L.A. Comic Con attendees across three floors of activations, featuring themed environments, live performances, and VIP exclusive lounges. Designed to feel like stepping into a living comic universe, Portalverse offered attendees, creators, and fans a new dimension of the Comic Con experience where cosplay met nightlife, and community met celebration.

The goal was to give L.A. Comic Con attendees a dynamic social destination outside of the Los Angeles Convention Center one that encouraged networking, self-expression, and connection while celebrating fandom culture in a high-energy, premium atmosphere.

Highlights

What we delivered

  1. 01

    Attendees included content creators, cosplayers, and entertainment professionals.

  2. 02

    Three floors of activations, including themed photo zones, live entertainment, and a VIP exclusive level with premium bar and lounge access.

  3. 03

    Immersive production design inspired by sci-fi, fantasy, and pop culture aesthetics.

  4. 04

    Live DJ sets and curated performances that captured the electric energy of Comic Con's creative community.

  5. 05

    Vendor and sponsor integrations featuring collectible merchandise, themed cocktails, and branded installations.

  6. 06

    Significant social media engagement before, during, and after the event, building excitement and extending the reach of L.A. Comic Con's brand.

  7. 07

    Established the closing as an annual cultural highlight, giving attendees a place to unwind, network, and celebrate in style.

Impact

What it changed

  1. 01

    Expanded L.A. Comic Con's footprint beyond the convention center, creating a new marquee nightlife experience tied directly to the brand.

  2. 02

    Enhanced attendee satisfaction and engagement, offering fans a high-quality space to connect, network, and celebrate fandom culture after hours.

  3. 03

    Elevated the convention's cultural relevance, positioning L.A. Comic Con alongside other major pop-culture events that offer premium, immersive after-dark programming.

  4. 04

    Boosted visibility and social media impact, generating substantial user-generated content from influencers, cosplayers, and creators.

  5. 05

    Strengthened relationships between L.A. Comic Con and its creator community, providing a shared physical space for collaboration and connection.

  6. 06

    Set the foundation for Portalverse as an annual signature event, adding long-term value to the L.A. Comic Con brand and attendee experience.

  7. 07

    Demonstrated RASA's capability to produce large-scale genre and fandom-driven experiences, creating momentum for future collaborations with entertainment and pop-culture partners.
