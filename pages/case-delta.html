Case study · Miami

DELTA Celebrating 80 Years of Service in Miami

An immersive, high-touch anniversary event showcasing Delta's excellence, reach, and hospitality.

Overview

Client
Delta Air Lines
Location
Miami
Audience
Delta executives, high-profile business associates, global partners (Air France, KLM, LATAM, AeroMexico, Virgin Atlantic), celebrities, and the Mayor of Miami
Scope
Venue scouting and negotiation, creative direction, full-scale production, décor, entertainment booking, stage and AV design, lighting, curated menus, branded moments, gifting, and guest experience management

Delta Air Lines partnered with RASA World Group, Inc. to produce Celebrating 80 Years of Service in Miami, an elevated milestone event honoring Delta's eight decades of innovation, service, and partnership in the region.

Designed for an audience of global airline partners, top executives, and civic leaders, the evening celebrated Delta's enduring legacy through an elegant and immersive experience that merged aviation heritage with contemporary luxury.

RASA's team oversaw the entire creative and operational execution, from venue scouting and contract negotiation to set design, staging, entertainment, and guest experience curation. The event environment was brought to life through custom branded décor, dynamic lighting design, and micro-environments themed to Delta's global alliance partners, showcasing the airline's international reach and commitment to connection.

The evening featured a curated culinary experience and signature cocktails designed to showcase the global partners, plus live entertainment to complement the refined atmosphere. Every detail from step & repeat to a gifting suite, was orchestrated to deliver a seamless, world-class experience that reflected Delta's excellence and hospitality.
