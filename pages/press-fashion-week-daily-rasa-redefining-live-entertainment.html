RASA Is Redefining Live Entertainment, And You’re Invited to Experience It at Six Flags Magic Mountain

March 15, 2025 · By Tom White

In the ever-evolving landscape of entertainment, one name is making waves by transforming iconic spaces into immersive, culture-shaping experiences: RASA World, Inc. Founded in 2021 by three Silicon Valley entrepreneurs, RASA has rapidly become a global phenomenon, known for crafting unforgettable gatherings that blend music, technology, and community.

What began as a series of high-energy networking events in San Francisco has expanded across the world, from Los Angeles to London, Dubai, and beyond. At its core, RASA’s success lies in its ability to seamlessly merge cutting-edge technology with the art of experience-making. Partnering with powerhouse brands like Six Flags, The Santa Monica Pier, and Playboy, RASA isn’t just throwing events, it’s redefining how we celebrate, connect, and engage with the world around us.

Now, the entertainment disruptor is bringing its signature magic back to LA for one night only. THRILL CITY by RASA, a nocturnal takeover of Six Flags Magic Mountain, promises to be an electrifying collision of music, nostalgia, and adrenaline.

Set to take place on March 22 from 8 PM to 3 AM, this exclusive event will transform the legendary amusement park into a dreamlike playground where the rides don’t stop, the music never fades, and the energy is unmatched. With six iconic roller coasters running through the night and a festival-style lineup of 15 world-class DJs, THRILL CITY is more than just an event, it’s a statement.

Expect performances from A-Trak, Birds of Mind, Colyn, Darius, DJ Harvey, Lara G, Rony Seikaly, and more, spread across three uniquely curated stages. Whether you’re dancing under the stars or catching your breath in an exclusive VIP lounge, every moment at THRILL CITY is designed to thrill.

“THRILL CITY is about more than just entertainment, it’s about reimagining the way we experience culture, music, and community,” says Ahmad Muhaisen, CEO and Co-Founder of RASA. “By bringing people together in iconic locations, we’re creating something that extends beyond a single night. This is about building a movement.”

With VIP lounges, private bars, and luxury table reservations available, THRILL CITY offers a chance to experience Six Flags like never before. If you’re ready for the ultimate fusion of festival energy and amusement park nostalgia, this is your sign to grab your ticket now.
