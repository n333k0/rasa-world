After-Hours EDM Music Festival Coming to Six Flags Magic Mountain

March 21, 2025 · By Christine Samra

RASA is taking over Six Flags Magic Mountain for Thrill City.

“For the second year in a row, RASA is bringing high-speed roller coasters, top DJs, immersive art installations, and immersive experiences to one of the most legendary amusement parks,” RASA said.

Attendees can ride X2, Tatsu, Full Throttle, Viper, The New Revolution, Ninja and The Grand Carousel while vibing to beats from house and techno DJs on stages placed around the park. A-Trak, Birds of Mind, Colyn, Darius, DJ Harvey, Lara G, Malas, Masha Mar B2b Heidi Lawden, Mia Moretti, Parallelle, Rhye, Rony Seikaly, Shimza and WhoMadeWho are all set to perform.

Art installations will be available from Olivia Steele, Maejor, Secular Sabbath, Mr Dallars, Punkmetender, Liquid Pxl and more.

Guests can enjoy a full bar with handcrafted cocktails and mocktails along with elevated food trucks. The event is for those age 21 and up.

Six Flags Season Pass holders and Single Day Tickets do not grant entry to Thrill City as this is a separate event.

RASA: Thrill City takes place on March 22 from 8:00 p.m. to 3:00 a.m.
