Case study · H. Lorenzo, Los Angeles, CA

H. Lorenzo: Mowalola x Miss Sixty Launch

A late-night warehouse rave where fashion, music, and raw sensuality collided to launch a collaboration.

Overview

Client
Mowalola x Miss Sixty, presented by H. Lorenzo
Location
H. Lorenzo, Los Angeles, CA
Audience
Fashion community, tastemakers, and press drawn from LA's design and nightlife scene
Scope
Full-scale event production, creative direction, venue transformation, talent and performer relations, lighting and technical production, beverage activations, and onsite operations.

RASA produced the launch of the Mowalola x Miss Sixty collaboration, staged inside a raw Los Angeles warehouse slated to become H. Lorenzo's archive store in 2025.

RASA transformed the industrial shell into a full-throttle rave environment, an immersive nightlife world built to channel the collection's equestrian-inspired, provocative energy. Pole performers worked the floor beside the DJ booth, laser lighting cut through the space during reggaeton-charged sets, and premium mezcal service from Rosaluna and Luxardo kept the crowd immersed into the early hours.

The evening brought together designer Mowalola, the fashion community, and press for a performance-driven showcase, with Mowalola herself taking the stage in pieces from the collaboration. Positioned as both a product launch and a cultural moment, the event fused runway, club, and brand experience into a single high-impact night, reinforcing both labels' boundary-pushing identity and activating H. Lorenzo's future archive space before its public debut.

Highlights

What we delivered

  1. 01

    Complete transformation of a raw industrial warehouse into an immersive, club-forward rave environment.

  2. 02

    Live musical program featuring Mowalola, Chi, KuntFetish, DJ G2G, TECHGRL, Hudat, and Shigecki.

  3. 03

    Choreographed pole performances staged beside the DJ booth for a charged, high-energy atmosphere.

  4. 04

    Custom laser and lighting design synchronized to reggaeton and electronic sets.

  5. 05

    Premium beverage activations with Rosaluna mezcal and Luxardo.

  6. 06

    A live performance by designer Mowalola showcasing looks from the collection.

  7. 07

    Seamless production delivered inside an unfinished venue, timed to H. Lorenzo's forthcoming archive space.
