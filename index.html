The concept

RASA, and the RASA World.

RASA is a Los Angeles live-experience company. It designs brand experiences, from targeted activations to full productions, and stays accountable for strategy, venues, execution and ROI. It works with Google, Delta, Playboy, Rivian and Aston Martin, among others.

RASA World is how the site tells that story. RASA isn't a list of services; it's one ecosystem. The page opens on a film that closes into a world. Three moons orbit it, one for each arm. Its original IPs are universes you step into through portals. A decade of moments forms a wall, a ring or a sphere. At the close, the world gathers its galaxy: the community.
