# RASA World concept: brief (owner, 2026-11-12)

The owner asked, in chat and in Spanish (summarised here):

- Build a RASA World page (https://rasaworld.com/), another brand, using the Not Human landing (RemotoLabs-aios, `not-human-v10.html`) as the base: the header that shrinks into a pill, the full-screen layout, the way content is shown, and all its features.
- Open with video.
- At some point, use the WebGL glass object ("textura webshell") from Not Human: shapes that shrink, change and travel across the whole page.
- Make the 3D fit the name: a world that turns, with different universes.
- Use all of RASA's content, presented better than the current site does: the video, the logos, Three Arms / One RASA Universe, Thrill City, Pier Play, Portalverse, Monuments, the photos, Explore Our Work. Everything, full screen.
- Adjust the tone so it fits the brand.
